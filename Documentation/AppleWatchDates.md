## Important distinctions

- Original Apple Watch was announced on 2014-09-09, not at the 2015-03-09 pricing/availability event; initial customer deliveries began 2015-04-24
- Series 1 and Series 2 share 2016-09-16 availability; this is explicitly confirmed by Apple's pricing-and-availability section and contemporary reporting
- Series 7 was announced 2021-09-14, but its first availability was 2021-10-15; 2021-10-08 was the preorder date
- Ultra arrived 2022-09-23, one week after Series 8 and SE 2
- Ultra 2 retains its 2023 launch dates; the 2024 black finish did not introduce a new hardware definition
- Series 12 and Ultra 4 dates were verified against their 2026 Apple announcements and contemporary reviews rather than extrapolated from annual launch patterns
- Later Nike/Hermès collections and later regional cellular rollouts are deliberately not substituted for the first availability of the hardware family
