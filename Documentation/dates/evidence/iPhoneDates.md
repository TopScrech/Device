# iPhone announcement and availability audit

Rechecked 2026-10-20: individually compared announcement and release fields for all 55 definitions with source evidence and the Swift catalogue

Verified 2026-10-19 against Apple Newsroom and a second publisher for each of the 55 model definitions

Dates use the calendar date stated by the source, not a conversion of the event time into the reader’s time zone

Announcement means the first official public presentation; release means first general customer availability in any market, excluding preorder starts

Each definition describes a product family: later colors, storage capacities, CDMA variants, regional A-models and country rollouts do not change its earliest launch date

## Evidence notes

- Original iPhone: the January announcement specified only June; the exact June 29 release is verified by [Apple’s June 28 launch notice](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/06/28iPhone-Premieres-This-Friday-Night-at-Apple-Retail-Stores/) and [MacRumors launch-day coverage](https://www.macrumors.com/2007/06/29/iphone-now-at-apple-store/)
- iPhone 4: June 24 is the original product launch, not the later white or CDMA model launch
- iPhone X: announced alongside iPhone 8 on September 12, 2017, but first available November 3 rather than September 22
- iPhone XR: September 12, 2018 announcement and October 26 availability differ from the XS family’s September 21 availability
- iPhone 12 family: October 13, 2020 announcement applies to all four models; October 23 availability applies to 12 and 12 Pro, while November 13 applies to mini and Pro Max
- iPhone 14 Plus: announced September 7, 2022 with the other models, but October 7 availability differs from their September 16 release
- iPhone 15 and 16 families: the second publisher’s historical release table is used, not its speculative iPhone 17 predictions
- iPhone Duo: October 23, 2026 is Apple’s officially scheduled first availability and is still in the future at this audit date; October 16 is only the preorder date, and October 30 is a later regional rollout
- No dates in this iPhone catalogue were copied from the app’s previous release-date mapping or inferred from the hardware identifier
