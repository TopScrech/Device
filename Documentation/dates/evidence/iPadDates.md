# iPad announcement and availability audit

Rechecked 2026-10-20: individually compared announcement and release fields for all 45 definitions with source evidence and the Swift catalogue

Checked 2026-10-19 against Apple material and a second source for every definition

Release means the earliest documented general consumer availability, not necessarily every isolated early delivery
Dates describe the product model family, not every regional SKU, later storage capacity, or hardware revision within its identifiers; first availability in any launch market is used
Wi-Fi and cellular identifiers grouped in one definition share the earliest launch, which may be the Wi-Fi variant
Dates are local civil dates from the launch market, not UTC conversions of article publication timestamps
Orders and preorders are not treated as physical availability; isolated premature deliveries or review units do not establish a general launch

## Unresolved exact availability

## Interpretation notes

- Apple often specified only next week at presentation; where so, the second source establishes the exact retail/delivery day, and the Apple evidence is described as a window rather than an exact date
- The original iPad announcement promised late March, superseded by Apple's March 5 April 3 launch notice
- iPad 2 includes the later `iPad2,4` revision; the model-family date remains the original iPad 2 launch, not that revision's introduction
- iPad (5th generation) uses the independently confirmed March 30 retail launch; March 24 was orders, and some tables also use March 31 estimates instead
- iPad (6th generation) uses March 30, not the March 27 announcement/order date
- Air 3 and mini 5 use March 26 based on Verizon's launch notice reproduced by a third-party publisher; Apple only supplied the week, and Apple's own personal pickup came March 29

- iPad (7th generation) uses September 26, the first documented retail pickup date; September 25 in common release-date tables is Apple’s shipment-start date, not a confirmed customer delivery date, and September 30 was the superseded original plan

The 2026-10-20 recheck also found [Yodobashi’s September 17 Japanese release listing](https://www.yodobashi.com/ec/promotion/newtopics/detail/60000000000000004521/) and [September 17 retail coverage](https://akiba-pc.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/news/721805.html) for iPad mini 4 — these establish later Japanese availability; the follow-up search found the September 16 US retail purchases documented above
