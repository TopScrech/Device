The 2026-10-20 follow-up found two May 30 reports identifying purchased units: [Sunday Gamer](https://sundaygamer.net/ipod-touch-7/) lists the purchase date, Apple online store, price and photographs; [Frankie Tech's unboxing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NcugWoep9Yw), published May 30, explicitly identifies Apple.com as the purchase source

May 30 is therefore the earliest documented consumer availability found, consistent with [S-MAX's May 29 report](https://s-max.jp/archives/1772929.html) of earliest expected Japanese delivery on May 30

Confidence limitation: [AppleInsider had a unit on May 29](https://appleinsider.com/articles/19/05/29/2019-ipod-touch-first-look-initial-impressions-benchmarks/amp/), but does not identify whether it was purchased, delivered to a consumer or supplied for review; it cannot establish or exclude consumer availability that day
The recorded May 30 date is an evidence-based inference under the catalogue's earliest-documented-availability convention, not a verified Apple worldwide retail launch date
